[**Maurice Kraus**](https://www.ml.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/people/mkraus/index.html) is a Ph.D. student at the AIML Lab Computer Science Department of TU Darmstadt in Germany. His research is dedicated to explaining depp models in the context of multimodal time series data. More specifically, he works on techniques that enable humans to understand the reasoning behind the predictions made by machine learning models when they operate on complex and diverse time-varying data types, such as images, videos, audio, and text, that are collected over time. His work includes time series models such as [xLSTM Mixer](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.16928) and novel benchmarks like [QuAnTS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.05124), for question-answering on time series.
